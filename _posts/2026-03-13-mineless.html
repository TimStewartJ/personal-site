Mineless

A graph-based factory automation game set in interconnected cave systems.

The Idea

Most factory games are spatial: belts, grids, layouts, and physical constraints. Mineless asks what happens if the core puzzle is topology instead. Caves are nodes, pathways are edges, and the interesting decisions come from flow, capacity, processing, and graph structure.

Core Concepts

  • Cave nodes connected by pathways instead of a traditional factory grid
  • Miners as mobile, versatile workers and machines as stationary specialists
  • Node capacity based on cave size
  • A tech tree that moves from basic extraction to automation, advanced logistics, and expansion
  • An in-world assistant, AIME, to help guide players through systems

Why It Interests Me

Mineless sits in the overlap between programming instincts and game design. It is about making a system that is readable, optimizable, and playful without requiring every interesting decision to happen on a square grid.

Current State

The next milestone is a playable demo with one complete gameplay loop: mine, process, export, and understand why the system works.

Stack

Unity, targeting PC.
