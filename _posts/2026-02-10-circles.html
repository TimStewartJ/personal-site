Circles

Collaborative journaling for private groups.

The Problem

Most journaling apps are built for solo reflection. That is valuable, but many of the best conversations happen when people reflect together: small groups, close friends, ministry teams, or families trying to build a shared habit.

The Solution

Circles gives groups a private place to respond to daily prompts, share reflections, and look back on their history. The goal is not to make journaling social in the noisy-feed sense. It is to make reflection easier to start, easier to sustain, and easier to bring into real conversations.

Current State

The core app is functional and in personal use. Circles, daily prompts, entry sharing, history views, and streaks are working. The current strategic question is whether it should become a public product, an open-source project, or remain a focused tool for a smaller community.

Stack

TanStack, Vercel, Azure Cosmos DB, and Google OAuth.
